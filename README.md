【世界観】

西暦4489年――。
科学技術とアンドロイドが社会を支配する超未来世界。
かつてイギリスのマンチェスターにあるアストラ研究機関（ARI）は、人間に超常の力を与える奇跡の物質――**「アストロ」**の開発に成功した。
しかし4436年。
開発者である天才科学者 クロノス博士 は、研究データとサンプルを犯罪組織へ売り渡し、そのまま姿を消す。
やがてアストロ結晶体の模造品 「スターダスト」 が闇市場に流出。
異能を手にした犯罪者たちは世界中で暗躍し、各国の治安は急速に悪化していった。
一方で、この技術には隠された真実があった。
それは、イギリス政府の諜報機関 MI5 と アストラル財団 が極秘に進めていた最高機密――
『愛国者計画（パトリオット・プロジェクト）』。
その目的は、国家の脅威となる凶悪犯罪者や国際指名手配犯を、異能を持つ特殊工作員によって排除することだった。
だが、失われた研究データと拡散したスターダストによって、その計画は世界規模の混乱へと発展する。
闇に消えたクロノス博士の行方。
各国で頻発する異能犯罪。
これは、未来都市ロンドンを舞台に、
探偵、諜報員、犯罪者、そして異能者たちが織りなす、
「科学」と「陰謀」と「異能」が交錯する近未来サスペンスアクションである

### English version

Detailed Description

You are the assistant to the brilliant detective Charlotte!
As Charlotte’s trusted partner, you will investigate and solve difficult cases that occur primarily in the United Kingdom, as well as mysterious incidents around the world.
Rather than being a traditional mystery focused purely on deduction, the story combines detective work, investigation, and supernatural battles, creating an action-oriented mystery centered around individuals with extraordinary abilities.

World Setting

The year is 4489 AD.
Human civilization has entered an ultra-futuristic age dominated by advanced science, artificial intelligence, and androids.
Years ago, the Astra Research Institute (ARI) in Manchester, England, successfully developed a miraculous substance known as “Astro,” capable of granting supernatural abilities to humans.
However, in 4436, the brilliant scientist responsible for its development, Dr. Chronos, betrayed the institute. He sold classified research data and Astro samples to criminal organizations before disappearing without a trace.
Before long, an artificial imitation of Astro crystals known as “Stardust” began circulating throughout the black market.
Criminals empowered by Stardust emerged across the world, using their newly acquired supernatural abilities to commit increasingly dangerous crimes. As a result, public security rapidly deteriorated across numerous countries.
But there was another truth hidden behind the technology.
The British intelligence agency MI5, together with the Astral Foundation, had secretly been conducting a highly classified operation known as the “Patriot Project.”
Its objective was to use special operatives possessing supernatural abilities to eliminate dangerous criminals, international fugitives, and other individuals considered serious threats to national security.
However, the disappearance of the original research data and the worldwide spread of Stardust caused the project to spiral into a crisis far beyond anyone’s control.
Where has Dr. Chronos disappeared to?
Who is responsible for the supernatural crimes erupting across the world?
And what secrets are still hidden behind Astro and the Patriot Project?
Set primarily in the futuristic metropolis of London, this is a near-future supernatural suspense-action story in which detectives, intelligence agents, criminals, and ability users become entangled in a vast web of science, conspiracy, and supernatural powers.
